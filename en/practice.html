Practice

Our practice is organized best to facilitate the work with international clients.

We strictly follow our high quality standards to exceed client expectations. We work hard on optimizing our work processes and streamlining the practice in order to let our clients benefit from lower costs.

We run a paperless electronic office which enables us and our clients to get access to tax documents and information any time and any place.

By using all modern channels we make sure that we communicate efficiently with our clients around the world

  • E-mail availability around the clock
  • Telephone and video conferences
  • Data transfer via FTP

We work with tailored checklists to collect information from our clients in a most efficient way. But we set high value on the personal coordination with the client.

We permanently improve our tax skills in international taxation and other areas.

By attending workgroups and staying in touch with professional associations and the tax administration we keep ourselves up-to-date in current legislation procedures.

We conduct tax presentations and distribute newsletter for our clients on actual topics and developments in the German tax law.
