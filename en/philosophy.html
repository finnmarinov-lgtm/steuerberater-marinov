Philosophy

We want to contribute to your success with our quality service.

We measure the quality of our service with your satisfaction and success. The practice owner and his team has developed a strong service orientation during the years of work with foreign consultants and clients which are service providers in other competitive sectors. We identify ourselves primarily as service provider and seek to impress our clients with excellent service.

Our consultants and cooperation partners have mostly long experience in international taxation and will support you to find local and global solutions.

Working close with you

We spend as much time as needed to understand your situation in order to offer you services tailored to your needs. We always prefer the direct and straight-forward communication and meeting/talking our clients in person. We meet our clients at their premises if the prefer so. We offer bring and collect service for documents on demand.

Single point of contact

We communicate with one and the same consultant any time. You do not have to deal with changing contacts.

Quick Processing

We work hard to finish each project as quick as possible and reach the expected results. Even if we cannot keep a very short deadline we seek alternative solutions based on our client's schedule.

Transparent work

We keep our clients posted at any stage of the work. Our communication efficiency and modern IT systems make this possible without causing additional fees for the client.

International network

We offer assistance in non-German tax issues with our extended network of foreign consultants in all developed nations some and various developing countries. Our cooperation partners have expertise in the area of international taxation.

Individual Fees

We adapt our fees to the individual scope of service for each client. We communicate and discuss with the client openly our fee and invoicing conditions from the very beginning to avoid later misunderstandings and "surprises" for both sides. When invoicing we stick strictly to the agreed fees. We practice immediate invoicing.
