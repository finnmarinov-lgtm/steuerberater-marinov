Welcome to the website of Marinov & Partner Steuerberater!

We focus on consulting for international employees (mostly expatriates) as well as for their employers. We have big international groups among our clients but also small and medium-sized German and foreign companies with cross-border activities. The main purpose of our advice is to optimize the client's tax and social security situation in all countries involved. The full avoidance of double taxation is our primary objective.

The partners Emil and Nadine Marinov have been working at the Frankfurt department for expatriate taxation of a Big Four company for several years.

We advise clients in many countries all over the world.

We enjoy working in person with people from different countries and nations which is the most important piece of our philosophy.

Who we work for

  • ExpatriatesTax and social security during assignments abroad – from the first meeting to the last tax return.
  • HR managersPlanning assignments, reviewing contracts and handling wage tax and social security correctly.
  • Freelancers and companiesSet-up, accounting, annual statements and ongoing tax advice – with or without an international angle.
  • Permanent moversWhat to consider for tax purposes when moving to or from Germany for good.
