Permanent movers

General advice

  • Initial Consultations upon the tax and social security impact of the residence move.
  • International tax planning (income tax, inheritance tax, VAT/Customs duty etc.)
  • Hypothetical tax calculations and other simulations
  • Clarification of personal tax status and obligations with tax authorities
  • Registration support with local institutions

Wage tax withholdings / Capital tax withholdings

  • Applications for exemption from wage tax withholding according to tax treaties
  • Applications for reduction/cancellation of the monthly wage tax withholdings
  • Applications for upfront exemption from capital tax withholdings
  • Application for refund of withheld capital taxes according to tax treaties

Social security

  • Arrangement for A1 (formerly E101) and other certificates of coverage
  • Application for refund of paid social security contributions
  • Child benefit (Kindergeld) and parental benefit (Elterngeld) applications

Personal tax returns

  • Income tax returns for residents and non-residents
  • Tax returns for determination of joint income
  • VAT monthly report / VAT annual tax returns
  • Inheritance and gift tax returns

Procedural support

  • Tax office queries to tax returns and other tax affairs
  • Negotiation of binding agreements and rulings with German tax authorities
  • Verification of tax assessment notices
  • Filing of appeals with the tax authorities and representation in tax court lawsuits
  • Tax audits assistance
  • Support with voluntary disclosures and tax criminal proceedings
