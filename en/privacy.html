Privacy policy

This statement explains which personal data are processed when you visit this website and what rights you have. In short: the website does not set cookies, does not use analytics or advertising services and does not load content from third-party servers.

2. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the website, GitHub processes the data that are technically necessary to deliver it, in particular your IP address, the date and time of the request and the page requested; your browser usually also transmits information about the browser and operating system. According to GitHub, the IP address of visitors to a GitHub Pages site is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub decides how long these data are kept. We do not receive these logs and do not analyse them.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to provide the website securely and reliably.

GitHub, Inc. is based in the USA. According to GitHub, it participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; for transfers to certified companies in the USA there is an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45 GDPR). For more information, see GitHub’s privacy statement.

3. No cookies, no analytics, no third-party content

This website does not set cookies and does not use any analytics, tracking or advertising services. Fonts, program libraries and the map data for the globe are stored on the same server as the website. No connections to other providers, such as font services or content delivery networks, are made when you visit it.

4. Settings stored in your browser

To remember your settings, the website stores a few items exclusively in your browser on your device:

  • the colour scheme you chose (dark, light or match device) and the text size – in the browser’s local storage, once you change the setting, until you delete them;
  • a note that you have already seen the camera move on the home page or have already visited other pages, so that you go straight to the globe when you return to the home page – in the browser’s session storage, which is deleted when you close the window or tab.

These items are not transmitted to us or anyone else. You can delete them at any time in your browser settings. The legal basis is Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG (German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act), as the storage is necessary to display the website the way you requested.

5. Tax news from Haufe

The reports under “Tax news from Germany” come from the public news feed of Haufe (haufe.de). An automated process at GitHub (GitHub Actions) retrieves them once a day and stores them as a file on the server of this website. Your browser therefore does not connect to Haufe when you visit our website. Only when you click on a report does the Haufe website open in a new window; Haufe’s privacy policy applies there.

6. Links to other websites

The website contains links to other providers, for example to OpenStreetMap for directions and to authorities and chambers. Data are only transmitted to these providers when you click on a link. The respective providers are responsible for their content and data processing.

7. Contacting us by e-mail, phone or fax

If you contact us by e-mail, phone or fax, we process the information you provide (such as your name, contact details and the content of your enquiry) in order to deal with your enquiry. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your enquiry relates to an engagement or its preparation, and otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in answering enquiries). We delete the data as soon as they are no longer needed for this purpose, unless statutory retention obligations apply.

Data transmission over the internet, for example by e-mail, may have security gaps. You can therefore also send us information by other means, for example by phone.

8. Job applications

If you apply for a job with us, for example by e-mail, we process your information to carry out the application procedure (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). If an employment relationship is established, we keep the documents for the employment relationship in accordance with the statutory provisions. Otherwise, we delete the application documents two months after notifying you of the rejection, unless other legitimate interests prevent this, such as a burden of proof in proceedings under the German General Equal Treatment Act (AGG).

9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16 GDPR), erasure (Art. 17 GDPR), restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR) and data portability (Art. 20 GDPR). If you have given consent, you can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR). Simply contact us (see section 1).

Right to object: Where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR). The authority responsible for us is:

Landesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz Sachsen-Anhalt (State Commissioner for Data Protection of Saxony-Anhalt)
Otto-von-Guericke-Straße 34a
39104 Magdeburg
Germany
datenschutz.sachsen-anhalt.de

10. No automated decision-making

We do not use automated individual decision-making or profiling.

11. Changes

We update this statement when the website or the law changes. Last updated: October 2026.
